About the company

Bridging the gap between data and impact.

TerraINT Systems Pvt. Ltd. — short for Terrain Intelligence — empowers organizations with geospatial and environmental solutions designed to turn raw data into decisions that hold up in the field.

We bring sensors, satellites, software and surveyors together under one roof, so the answer to “what is happening, and where?” arrives as something you can act on.

Multi-domain coverage

IoT, satellite, GIS, survey and risk assessment — one partner for the full spatial picture.

Field-tested methods

Built on established geospatial and hydrological practice, not guesswork.

Decision-oriented output

Every dataset is structured to lead to a usable decision — maps, alerts and reports teams can act on.

Core services

SERVICE / 01

IoT Sensor-Based Solutions

Real-time monitoring and smart sensing systems for environmental, infrastructure and asset management — water level, flow, rainfall, groundwater, air and water quality.

Explore this service →
  • Continuous field data collection without manual visits
  • Weather, water, soil and structural sensor options
  • Cloud-connected dashboards and alerting
  • Battery / solar-powered units for remote installations
SERVICE / 02

Satellite-Based Products

Earth observation and remote sensing for monitoring, analysis and decision support at scale — from optical and multispectral imagery to all-weather SAR and value-added indices like NDVI.

Explore this service →
  • Wide-area coverage where ground access is limited
  • Change detection over weeks, months or years
  • Feeds directly into GIS and analytics workflows
  • Multispectral and radar imagery options
SERVICE / 03

GIS Software Licensing

Authorized access to industry-leading GIS platforms for mapping, spatial analysis, remote sensing and visualization — matched to what your projects actually need.

Explore this service →
  • Licensing for established, industry-standard platforms
  • Flexible options for individuals, teams and organizations
  • Easy renewals, upgrades and technical support
  • Training and onboarding support included
SERVICE / 04

Topographic Survey & Mapping

High-accuracy surveying and mapping for infrastructure, planning and development — including earthwork cut-and-fill analysis, mobile mapping and utility GIS.

Explore this service →
  • Land, boundary and engineering surveys
  • Drone photogrammetry and LiDAR-based terrain models
  • Deliverables ready for design and planning teams
  • Compliant with standard survey accuracy benchmarks
SERVICE / 05

Flood Risk Assessment

Scientific risk assessment and modelling that helps governments, businesses and communities build resilience — from drainage and flow-path analysis to industrial asset exposure mapping.

Explore this service →
  • Hydrological and hydraulic modelling of catchments
  • Hazard and exposure mapping for planning decisions
  • Supports early-warning and resilience planning
  • Reports formatted for policy and funding submissions
