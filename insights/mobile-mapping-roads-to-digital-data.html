Survey & Mapping

Mobile mapping: turning roads into digital data

A moving vehicle with LiDAR, GNSS, cameras and IMU can capture entire corridors in detail. What mobile mapping collects and how it is used.

TerraINT SystemsOct 20262 min read

Mobile mapping is a surveying technique that uses a moving vehicle fitted with LiDAR, GNSS, cameras and IMU sensors. Rather than measuring point by point, it captures large volumes of spatial data while driving through an area.

Multiple sensors, one survey

  • LiDAR captures dense 3D point clouds
  • Cameras record visual detail of roads and surroundings
  • GNSS and IMU provide precise position and movement

From road to digital map

The collected data is processed into 3D models, point clouds, road maps, elevation data and asset inventories — including signs, poles, buildings, utilities and other visible features.

Real roadsReal dataDigital insight

Where it is used

Road inventory, urban mapping, infrastructure inspection, asset management, transport planning and environmental surveys all benefit from fast, corridor-scale capture. The real value is a digital representation of the real world that can be analysed and kept up to date.
