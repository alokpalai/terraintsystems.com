IoT Sensing

Turning water data into real-time intelligence

River levels, rainfall and groundwater change constantly. Connected sensors make that change visible as it happens — and GIS shows where it matters.

TerraINT SystemsOct 20262 min read

Water systems never stand still. River levels rise, rainfall varies from one valley to the next, groundwater fluctuates through the seasons and water quality can shift quickly. Manual readings taken at intervals miss much of that story.

What can be monitored

  • Water level in rivers, lakes and reservoirs
  • Flow rate and discharge conditions
  • Rainfall for hydrological analysis
  • Groundwater level across locations
  • Water quality — pH, turbidity and temperature

How the system works

Sensors installed at rivers, reservoirs, rain gauges and wells collect readings continuously and send them to a cloud platform. There the data is analysed, and alerts are raised when thresholds are crossed.

SensorsTransmissionCloud platformAnalysisAlerts

Why location matters

Combined with GIS, every reading has a place on the map. Teams can see where levels are rising, where rainfall is concentrated and which areas need attention first — the difference between a spreadsheet of numbers and a decision.

Typical applications include flood early warning, water resource management, watershed monitoring, groundwater assessment and urban drainage planning.

The value isn't collecting more data. It's converting continuous observations into timely, location-based insight.
