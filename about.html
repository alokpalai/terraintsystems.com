About TerraINT

Terrain intelligence, built for decisions.

We are a geospatial and environmental consultancy from New Delhi, bringing sensors, satellites, software and surveyors together so organizations can see what is happening on the ground — and act on it.

What we do Get in touch
Our name
TerraTHE GROUNDINTINTELLIGENCE

TerraINT stands for Terrain Intelligence. The ground beneath every project — its levels, water, soil, assets and the way it changes — holds the answers to many decisions. Our job is to read it accurately and turn it into intelligence people can use.

Founded in 2025, TerraINT Systems Pvt. Ltd. delivers terrain intelligence and consultancy services with a global outlook.

// HOW WE WORK

  • Multi-domain coverage — IoT, satellite, GIS, survey and risk assessment under one roof.
  • Field-tested methods — built on established geospatial and hydrological practice.
  • Decision-oriented output — every dataset is structured to lead to a usable decision.
  • Customized solutions — domain knowledge applied to your specific site and question.
