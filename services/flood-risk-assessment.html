Overview

Is your facility prepared for the unexpected?

Unexpected flooding can damage critical assets, disrupt operations, interrupt supply chains and cause costly downtime. Exposure that goes unexamined today becomes a serious problem tomorrow.

TerraINT combines terrain and elevation analysis, satellite data, drainage modelling and GIS to show where water will go, what it will reach, and what to do about it — in formats ready for planning, policy and funding decisions.

    FAQ

    Common questions.

    Something else on your mind? Ask us directly →

    Who is flood risk assessment for?

    Governments, businesses and communities — especially industrial operators such as power plants, manufacturing, infrastructure, mining and logistics facilities.

    What data do you use?

    Typically terrain and elevation data, rainfall and hydrological data, land use and satellite imagery, combined in GIS. Where available, live IoT sensor data can support early warning.

    Can the report support funding or approvals?

    Yes. Reports can be formatted for policy and funding submissions.

    Can you combine this with ongoing monitoring?

    Yes. Assessment pairs naturally with our IoT sensing — water level and rainfall sensors can feed early-warning thresholds for the risks identified.
