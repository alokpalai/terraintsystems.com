Overview

Small sensors. Big impact.

Water systems, soils and structures change constantly. Traditional monitoring captures them only at intervals, which makes it hard to see what is actually happening between visits.

TerraINT deploys connected sensors across rivers, reservoirs, rainfall stations, groundwater wells, sites and assets. They collect data continuously and transmit it to a cloud platform for analysis and alerting — so changing conditions are seen the moment they happen.

How it works

Our process.

  1. Sense

    Sensors collect field data at the source.

  2. Transmit

    Networks move readings securely off-site.

  3. Store

    A cloud data platform keeps the full record.

  4. Analyse

    Dashboards and models track changing conditions.

  5. Alert

    Thresholds trigger alerts and insights for timely action.

FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly →

Can sensors work in remote places without mains power?

Yes. Battery- and solar-powered units are available for remote installations where grid power is not available.

Which parameters can you monitor?

Common options include water level, flow, rainfall, groundwater level, water quality (pH, turbidity, temperature), soil moisture, weather and structural or equipment condition. We recommend a set based on your objective.

Can the data feed into our own GIS or systems?

Yes. Data is collected on a cloud platform and can be exported to GIS and analytics workflows, so readings can be viewed by location alongside your other layers.

Do we get alerts when something changes?

Dashboards support alerting, so threshold crossings — such as a rising water level — can notify your team for timely action.
