Overview

Understand the terrain before the machines move it.

Every construction and development project starts with the ground. Accurate terrain data shapes design, quantities, cost and risk.

TerraINT combines GNSS and total-station survey, drone photogrammetry, LiDAR and mobile mapping to capture the ground accurately — then turns it into terrain models, maps and quantity reports that design and planning teams can use immediately.

How it works

Our process.

  1. Survey

    Capture field data with GNSS, total station, drone or LiDAR.

  2. Model

    Build a terrain model of the existing ground.

  3. Compare

    Run cut & fill against the design surface.

  4. Quantify

    Calculate volumes and net requirements.

  5. Report

    Detailed maps, drawings and reports.

FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly →

Which survey method is right for my site?

It depends on area, accuracy and terrain. Total station and GNSS suit detailed engineering work; drones cover open sites quickly; LiDAR and mobile mapping capture dense detail across larger areas and corridors. We recommend the mix during scoping.

Can you calculate cut and fill volumes?

Yes. We compare the existing ground model with your proposed design surface to calculate cut areas, fill areas and net volumes, with maps and reports.

What formats do deliverables come in?

Deliverables are prepared for design and planning teams — typically CAD and GIS-ready drawings, surface models, point clouds and reports.

Do you map utilities and infrastructure assets?

Yes. Utility and infrastructure GIS mapping brings roads, pipelines, drainage networks and power lines into a unified, layered spatial dataset.
