Overview

Seeing the Earth is easy. Understanding it is the work.

Land, water, infrastructure, agriculture and natural resources are changing quickly. Satellites observe all of it — but imagery on its own is just pixels.

TerraINT combines earth observation, remote sensing, GIS and analytics with multi-source spatial data to convert those observations into reliable maps, indicators and datasets that guide action.

FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly →

What is the difference between optical and SAR imagery?

Optical and multispectral sensors capture reflected light, which is ideal for vegetation, land use and visual interpretation. SAR (radar) works day and night and through cloud, which makes it valuable for floods, monsoon periods and deformation monitoring.

Can you show how an area has changed over time?

Yes. Change detection compares imagery across weeks, months or years to highlight where landscapes, settlements, forests or water bodies have changed.

Will the output work in our GIS?

Products are prepared to feed directly into GIS and analytics workflows.

Is satellite data useful where we cannot reach the site?

That is one of its biggest strengths — wide-area coverage where ground access is limited, unsafe or expensive.
